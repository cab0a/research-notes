and personal experimental use. Commercial use requires a separate written
license from the copyright holder. Previously published releases retain the
terms under which their copies were distributed.

## Historical Releases

Tagged releases v0.1.0 through v0.27.0 were distributed with a different
license. Changing the current project policy does not retroactively restrict
copies already obtained under those terms. Check out the applicable tag and
read the license included with that version when evaluating a historical
release. This section records history; it is not the policy for current or
future development.

## Permitted Noncommercial Use

The controlling terms are in [LICENSE](LICENSE). They permit noncommercial
purposes, specified personal research and experimentation without an
anticipated commercial application, and use by the noncommercial
organizations identified in the license. This summary does not replace or
modify those terms.

Public source availability does not make current or future versions open
source under the Open Source Definition because commercial fields of use are
not granted by the repository license.

## Commercial Licensing

Commercial use is available only under a separate written agreement. To start
a discussion, [open a GitHub issue](https://github.com/cab0a/research-notes/issues/new)
with `Commercial licensing inquiry` in the title. Do not include confidential,
personal, customer, or proprietary information in a public issue.

No commercial permission is granted merely by opening an issue or discussing
a proposed use. A separate written license must be agreed before commercial
use begins.

## Third-Party Material

This licensing policy covers only rights that the repository copyright holder
can grant. Dependencies, comparison parsers, standards material, and other
third-party content retain their own licenses and terms. A commercial
agreement for this repository does not grant rights in third-party material.

## Contributions

External code contributions are not currently accepted. Reproducible bug
reports, research questions, and source corrections may be proposed through
GitHub Issues without posting confidential or proprietary information. This
policy preserves a clear rights boundary for research publication and any
future commercial licensing discussion.
